Built for people who work a repeating pattern

Enter it once

Pick a preset such as 4 on 4 off, 2D 2N 4 off or Mon to Fri, or build your own cycle. Choose any date you know the pattern started and the preview shows the next two weeks, so you can check it against your real rota before you save.

Life changes, so can any day

Tap a day to swap a shift, mark a holiday or sick day, or leave a note like "Swapped with Sam". Edited days show a dot so you always know what differs from the pattern.

Glanceable, on purpose

Every shift type has its own colour, so a month reads at a glance in a car park at 6am. Light and dark mode, large text supported, and it works with VoiceOver.
